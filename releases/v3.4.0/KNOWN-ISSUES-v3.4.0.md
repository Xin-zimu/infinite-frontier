# V3.4.0 known issues

- Animals occupy stable one-tile positions in this version; roaming, pathfinding and animated movement remain future presentation work.
- A nearby animal-fence piece is the explicit breeding gate. V3.4 does not yet calculate a fully enclosed pen polygon.
- Husbandry advances by saved in-game day and deliberately adds no wall-clock offline production.
- Animal products use the shared 24-slot inventory; a full inventory keeps the complete ready quantity on the animal for a later retry.
- Animal presentation uses the project's generated vector-like draw layer rather than authored sprites and animation sets.
- Cooking, refining, equipment enhancement and automation are intentionally outside V3.4 scope.
