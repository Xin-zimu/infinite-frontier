# V2.6.0 known issues

- Equipment score uses the best qualifying items owned in the inventory; explicit worn equipment slots arrive with the later equipment-system milestone.
- Regional danger changes enemy combat values and elite chance but does not yet add tier-exclusive enemy species.
- Locked regional Boss map markers may be discovered before their encounter unlocks; the progression panel shows the exact requirement.
- Enemy level and elite presentation reuse the current pixel bodies, with a level-aware name and gold elite outline rather than bespoke variants.
- Region rewards are fixed by danger tier and can be claimed once per discovered region after their requirements are met.
