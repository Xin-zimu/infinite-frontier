# V1.7.0 known issues

- Dungeon entrances are world structures rather than exploration-map markers; map discovery belongs to V2.0.
- Dungeons use one deterministic six-room layout family in this release. Additional room archetypes and multi-floor variants remain later content.
- Dungeon walls, locks and encounter markers use programmatic validation art; the unified production art/audio pass remains scheduled for V4.11.
- Loot is transferred directly into the inventory. Equipment affixes and rarity begin in the V2.x progression series.
- Completed dungeons are revisit-safe but do not yet expose an in-world completion badge outside the dungeon HUD.
