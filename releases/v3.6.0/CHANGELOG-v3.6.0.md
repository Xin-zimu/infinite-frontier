# Infinite Frontier V3.6.0

## Equipment and enhancement

- Added six worn slots: weapon, helmet, chest, boots and two accessories.
- Added four quality tiers, four rarity tiers, deterministic affixes and the copper-guard set bonus.
- Added seven craftable equipment items, bringing the canonical catalogs to 68 items and 24 recipes.
- Added atomic inventory registration, equip/unequip, comparison, enhancement to +5 and repair.
- Composed equipment attack, defense, health and stamina into runtime player attributes.
- Preserved broken equipment at zero durability so it can be repaired.
- Added the responsive `O`-key equipment panel.
- Advanced save format to 23 with equipment-state schema 1 and explicit format-22 empty migration.
- Retained generation format 5 and its canonical checksum.
