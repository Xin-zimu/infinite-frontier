# V3.6.0 known issues

- Equipment is registered into a dedicated owned-item library and cannot be moved back into the 24-slot inventory in this version.
- Equipment visuals are represented in the interface and player statistics; character-paper-doll sprites are outside V3.6 scope.
- Quality, rarity and affixes are rolled once on registration and cannot be reforged.
- Enhancement always succeeds when materials are available; probabilistic upgrades are intentionally excluded.
- Timed production queues, conveyors and unloaded-machine logistics remain reserved for V3.7.
