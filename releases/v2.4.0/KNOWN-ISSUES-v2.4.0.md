# V2.4.0 known issues

- Faction shop stock is fixed per tier; timed shortages and replenishment arrive with dynamic world events.
- Control-point contests are progression APIs and archive status in this version; roaming capture encounters arrive in V2.5.
- Faction hostility affects standing, membership and services but does not yet launch coordinated world raids.
- The four faction identities are authored and fixed; random faction generation is outside the current roadmap.
- Standing changes have no wall-clock decay, preserving deterministic offline progression.
