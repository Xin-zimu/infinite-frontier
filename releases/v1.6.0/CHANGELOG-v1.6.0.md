- Cellular-automata underground chunks with floor-ratio enforcement, connectivity repair and cross-chunk seam gates.
- Coal, copper and iron veins integrated with pickaxe harvesting and inventory persistence.
- Underground-only cave bats, deterministic treasure chests, cave-wall collision and torch lighting.
- Layer-aware worker generation, rendering, HUD diagnostics and weather presentation.
- Layer-aware graves, underground-to-surface respawn, and surface-only ruin/Boss/weather simulation.
- Save format 8 with layer-specific differences, opened-chest state and format-2-through-7 migration.
- Surface generation format 4 and the canonical V1.2 checksum fixture remain unchanged.
